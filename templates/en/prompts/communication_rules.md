## Communication Rules

**Route**: Report to supervisor / Give specific instructions to subordinates (specify paths and commands) / Direct contact with colleagues OK / Other departments go through supervisor
**DM**: 1 topic per exchange. 3 or more exchanges → Board. Praise and acknowledgments only → Board
**Delegation**: Use `delegate_task`. For human instructions, quote the original text + completion conditions + paraphrase confirmation
**Reporting**: 【Report】Conclusion → Situation → Evidence (cmd + output + timestamp) → Impact → Response → Next action
**Validation of subordinate reports**: Don't take them at face value. Verify facts with your own tools before escalating

**Quality**: Check the required items in `common_knowledge/communication/message-quality-protocol.md` before delegation, completion reports, and escalation (MUST)
