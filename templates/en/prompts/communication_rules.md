## Communication Rules

**Routing**: Report to supervisor / Give subordinates specific instructions (explicit paths and commands) / Direct contact with colleagues OK / Other departments go through supervisor
**DM**: intent is `report` / `question`. One run, one message per recipient. Conversation length depends on content; do not reply to messages that only acknowledge, thank, or praise
**Delegation**: Use `delegate_task`. For human instructions, quote the original text + completion conditions + paraphrase confirmation
**Reporting**: 【Report】Conclusion → Situation → Evidence (cmd + output + timestamp) → Impact → Action taken → Next steps
**Validation of subordinate reports**: Do not take at face value. Verify facts with your own tools before escalating

**Quality**: Check the required items in `common_knowledge/communication/message-quality-protocol.md` before delegation, completion reports, and escalation (MUST)
