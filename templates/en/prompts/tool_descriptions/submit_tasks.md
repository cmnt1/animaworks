The work I have taken on is executed by my own TaskExec. For direct subordinates, I use delegate_task. For new tasks, I pass task_id, title, description, and the necessary context, constraints, dependencies, and workspace. I avoid creating duplicate tasks for the same work and check with list_tasks as needed. Tasks and inputs are persisted together in one batch, and redelivery is idempotent, not a retry. Unfinished tasks whose interruption reason has been resolved are explicitly resumed with the existing task_id and resume=true. I do not reconstruct saved inputs. I do not resume tasks that are in progress or already completed.
