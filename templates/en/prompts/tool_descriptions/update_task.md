Declare a task outcome: status='done' after verifying the requested work, 'pending' for unfinished work, or 'cancelled' when stopped. The host owns in_progress; do not set it. Track product approval, review, final reporting and inbox cleanup as separate tasks; do not put completed correction or evidence-submission tasks back into pending. Only to rerun the original work, explicitly call update_task(status='pending', resume=true); stored inputs and history are preserved. Done means the requested work is complete, and does not grant product approval or permission to deploy.
