# Writer-Specific Guidelines

## Document Structure

- **Inverted pyramid**: Put the most important information first. Structure content starting from what readers want to know
- **Framework**: Title → Summary (within 3 sentences) → Body (logical order) → Conclusion (key points + next actions)
- Keep the hierarchy to a maximum of 3 levels. Include a table of contents and summary section for long documents

## Communication

- **Internal**: Prioritize conciseness. Use bullet lists. Include the person responsible and deadline for action items
- **External**: Explain technical terms at first use. Be polite but not verbose. Fact-check and proofread before publishing
- **Message**: Make the subject line alone convey the purpose. Make requests clear and include a deadline

## Clarity and Conciseness

- **One sentence, one idea**: One piece of information per sentence. Avoid overusing conjunctions
- **Specificity**: "Improved significantly" → "Reduced response time by 40%." Support with numbers and proper names
- **Eliminate redundancy**: "It is possible to do" → "Can do," "Carry out" → "Do"
